# Review Checklist

Use when reviewing a deliverable rather than building one. Each item names the reference that settles a disputed call.

## Color — [color.md](color.md)

- A Slalom Blue is present, and no more than one secondary color appears alongside it.
- Every color comes from the palette, its approved tones and tints, or the neutrals.
- Text sits on an approved background combination.

## Typography — [typography.md](typography.md)

- The typeface matches the medium: Slalom Sans outside PowerPoint, Avenir Next LT Pro only in PowerPoint and the three Microsoft exceptions.
- Headlines and subheads are sentence case; body copy is flush left, ragged right.
- No more than three type sizes; no widows, orphans, or hyphenated line ends.

## Accessibility — [accessibility.md](accessibility.md)

- Text, charts, and data graphics pass WCAG 2.1 AA.
- Nothing communicates through color alone.
- The result still works in grayscale or low-color print if it might be printed.

## Logo — [logo.md](logo.md)

- The logo is one of the four approved colors, at least 0.125 in / 9 px tall, with clear space of at least an "o."
- No effects, rotation, skew, recoloring, or added elements.
- No new logo or mark was invented; any sub-brand is an approved lockup.

## Graphic elements — [graphic-elements.md](graphic-elements.md)

- Any color bar keeps the fixed order, appears once, and stays under 20% of the layout.
- Icons come from the approved library, in a single approved color, never reversed onto a dark background.
- Accents support the message instead of competing with it.

## Imagery — [imagery.md](imagery.md)

- Photography is bright, candid, globally relevant, and authentically human.
- No stock image depicts a Slalom employee, and documentary photos were not recolored in a way that changes meaning.
- Illustration is an accent, in black or a Slalom blue, with varied skin tones.

## Voice — [writing-style.md](writing-style.md), [creative-lens.md](creative-lens.md)

- Copy is clear, confident, and conversational, leading with people and customer impact.
- House style holds: sentence case, "customers" not clients, closed em dashes, numerals with `%`.
- "Fiercely human" appears intact and only as a descriptor.
- The whole thing reads people-first, uplifting, friendly, real, and innovative.
