# Creative Lens

Source: *Slalom Brand Guide*, February 2025, pp. 17-18. The one test that applies to every deliverable: **is it fiercely human?**

| Attribute | Voice | Visuals | Not |
|---|---|---|---|
| **People-first** | Approach technology stories as human stories. Lead with people and show the impact of what we do. Inclusive and globally sensitive. | Photos and illustrations showing the human benefit of technology. Personal connection at global scale. | technology-first, corporate, sentimental |
| **Uplifting** | Inspire others to build better tomorrows. Share knowledge generously, without pressure or fearmongering. | Bright, colorful, optimistic imagery. Helpful, easy-to-understand information design. | academic, authoritative, utopian |
| **Friendly** | Warm and conversational, with genuine desire to help. Curious and engaging. Don't sell — invite a relationship. | Globally relevant moments of empathy, connection, and collaboration. Playful custom illustration and color. | neutral, formal, sales-y, ingratiating |
| **Real** | Direct and clear. Simple language, minimal jargon. Honesty through humor that's humble, never mean. | Diverse but relatable people and scenes. Authentic, candid moments. Genuine emotion. | perfect, slick, posed, homogenous |
| **Innovative** | Creative and unexpected. Not the consultant you were expecting. Action-oriented, snappy headlines, concise summaries. | Dynamic compositions that symbolize transformation. Slalom lines evoking flexibility. Bite-sized, scannable text. | expected, conventional, chaotic, wacky |

Applying the lens: [writing-style.md](writing-style.md) for prose, [imagery.md](imagery.md) for photography and illustration, [messaging.md](messaging.md) for the "fiercely human" essence and its usage rules.
