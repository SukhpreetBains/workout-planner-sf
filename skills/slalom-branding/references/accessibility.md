# Accessibility

Source: *Slalom Brand Guide*, February 2025, pp. 38-39. Slalom conforms to WCAG 2.1 AA.

## Requirements

- All text, charts, and data graphics must strictly conform to WCAG 2.1 AA.
- Maintain at least `4.5:1` contrast for ordinary text and `3:1` for large text and meaningful chart elements.
- Never rely on color alone to convey meaning. Combine color with labels, patterns, shapes, outlines, or icons.

## Data visualization

- Label data directly, and distinguish series with fill textures or patterns as well as color.
- Coral Red and Chartreuse are difficult for color-blind readers — do not let them carry meaning on their own.
- Keep `4.5:1` between text and its background, including over two-tone patterns, and `3:1` between chart elements.
- Consider a 1 px top border on bar charts, or an outline on other chart types, to lift readability.

## Exemptions

Illustrations, icons, photos, and Slalom lines do not need to meet the text contrast standard. Give them alt text or captions instead.
