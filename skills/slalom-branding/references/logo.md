# Logo, Lockups, and Sub-branding

Source: *Slalom Brand Guide*, February 2025, pp. 24-32. Logo files: `assets/logos/`.

## Color

The Slalom logo may appear in exactly four colors: Slalom Blue `#0C62FB`, Slalom Dark Blue `#002FAF`, White `#FFFFFF`, and Black `#000000`. Never any secondary brand color, never a gradient, never a colorized or recolored variant.

- **Primary lockup:** Slalom Blue or Slalom Dark Blue on white. Prefer this.
- **Primary alternates,** in order of preference: on Slalom Blue, on Light Gray `#E6E6E6`, on Slalom Dark Blue. White is the preferred reversed color; Black is available.
- **Secondary backgrounds** (Cyan, Purple, Chartreuse): use a Slalom Blue or Slalom Dark Blue logo. On Coral Red or darker tones the logo may reverse out to white.
- **Black/White lockup:** reserved for black-and-white layouts.

## Size and clear space

- Minimum height 0.125 in / 9 px.
- Clear space on every side is at least the width of an "o" in the wordmark.

## Never

- Apply effects, shadows, outlines, or 3D.
- Rotate or skew.
- Change the color, or fill it with a pattern or texture.
- Combine it with other logos, words, graphics, photos, slogans, or symbols to form a hybrid mark.
- Stretch or redraw the wordmark.

## On photography

The logo may sit on colored, illustrated, or photographic backgrounds as long as legibility and integrity hold.

- Prefer photos already matching a single brand color with a simple background.
- Center-align, or corner-align when the image is complex.
- Do not place it over high-contrast, multicolor, or busy areas, over non-brand colors, or across subjects' faces.

## Sub-branding

**One logo. One global brand.** There are no secondary logos, and no new logo may be created for any product, service, technology, program, or team — no exceptions. The only permitted extension is a lockup pairing the Slalom logo with a descriptor.

Before proposing any sub-brand, answer three questions: does it uplevel the Slalom brand, could it increase brand confusion, and is the name clear, simple, and descriptive rather than abstract? Prefer `slalom human resources` or `slalom london` over an invented name like `slalom converge`.

### Lockup construction

All descriptor text is Slalom Sans Light at **-10 tracking**.

- **Horizontal:** descriptor begins at the midpoint of the logo's "m," set at the same x-height as the stem of the "a."
- **Stacked** (preferred for teams, locations, and industries): descriptor's top edge sits at the vertical midpoint of the "l," with its start point aligned to the top of the "s" in the lower curve.
- **Partner** (preferred for partner logos): partner mark begins at the full width of the logo's "m," separated by a divider line 125% the height and 10% the width of the logo's "l." The partner mark must not exceed the divider's height and is centered vertically on it.

### Employee Resource Groups

ERGs run a separate approved sub-branding system — gradient "S" mark, signature color, pattern, and icon per group (All My Relations, Horizons, Reach, Amplify, Military, Unidos, Aspire, Prism, Women). Refer to the ERG Style Guide rather than improvising ERG artwork.
