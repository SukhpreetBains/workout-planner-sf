# Color

Source: *Slalom Brand Guide*, February 2025, pp. 33-37, 44.

## Palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Slalom Blue | `#0C62FB` |
| Primary | Slalom Dark Blue | `#002FAF` |
| Secondary | Cyan | `#1BE1F2` |
| Secondary | Coral Red | `#FF4D5F` |
| Secondary | Purple | `#C7B9FF` |
| Secondary | Chartreuse | `#DEFF4D` |
| Neutral | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Neutral | Light Gray (10% black) | `#E6E6E6` |
| Neutral | Dark Gray, text only (60% black) | `#666666` |
| Neutral | Black | `#000000` |

Hyperlinks use Slalom Blue. Use the official color names — Slalom Dark Blue, Coral Red, Purple, Chartreuse — not Navy, Red, Lavender, or Lime.

**A Slalom Blue must be present in every composition.** Pair blue with only one secondary color per composition. Use black, white, and neutral grays for most text and surfaces, reserving saturated accents for emphasis, dividers, charts, section cues, and small graphic elements.

Use the published values rather than sampling with an eyedropper, which is not reliable. Print uses PMS or CMYK; digital uses RGB or hex. Slalom.com carries an extended accessibility palette defined in the Figma style guide — those colors are website-only and must not appear in any other digital or print collateral.

## Tones and tints

Each secondary color has three dark tones and three light tints.

| Color | Dark 3 | Dark 2 | Dark 1 | Core | Light 1 | Light 2 | Light 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cyan | `#00BAD6` | `#05C4DE` | `#0ED3EB` | `#1BE1F2` | `#5FEAF6` | `#8DF0F9` | `#D1F9FC` |
| Coral Red | `#E6154A` | `#F1214B` | `#F53958` | `#FF4D5F` | `#FF828F` | `#FFA6AF` | `#FFDBDF` |
| Purple | `#9488F0` | `#A099F8` | `#B6ACF9` | `#C7B9FF` | `#D8CEFF` | `#E3DCFF` | `#F4F1FF` |
| Chartreuse | `#B3CC3E` | `#BDDD07` | `#CBEB0F` | `#DEFF4D` | `#E8FF82` | `#EFFFA6` | `#F8FFDB` |

Primary and secondary colors should always lead. Use tones and tints for tone-on-tone depth, rarely as full floods and rarely as the dominant color in a layout. Do not introduce unapproved colors merely because they appear in a legacy example.

## Approved composition combinations

- One secondary core color and/or unlimited neutrals on a Slalom Blue background.
- One secondary core color and/or unlimited neutrals on Slalom Blue and/or neutrals.
- One secondary color's own tones and tints, plus neutrals, on Slalom Blue and/or one secondary color.
- A Slalom Blue and/or neutral on a Slalom Blue and/or neutral background.
- All brand colors at once **only** in infographic charts, bar graphs, and the official color bar. Anything else needs Global Marketing approval.

## Approved text combinations

These carry the accessibility requirement as well as the brand one. See [accessibility.md](accessibility.md).

| Background | Approved text colors |
|---|---|
| White | Slalom Blue, Slalom Dark Blue, Black, Dark Gray, Coral Red (Dark 3 Red at small sizes) |
| Slalom Blue | White, Chartreuse, Purple, Cyan |
| Black | White, Chartreuse, Purple, Cyan, Coral Red |
| Cyan, Purple, Chartreuse | Slalom Dark Blue, Black |
| Coral Red | White, Black |

Pair only one secondary color with Slalom Blue, Slalom Dark Blue, White, and/or Black at a time.
