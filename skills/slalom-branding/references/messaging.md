# Messaging and Boilerplate

Source: *Slalom Brand Guide*, February 2025, pp. 5-16. Use these as the approved wording when a deliverable needs to describe Slalom. Confirm headcount and office figures on SlalomOne before publishing them.

## Who we are

> Slalom is a fiercely human business and technology consulting company that teams with leaders who expect more. So we bring more.

**Purpose:** We help people and organizations dream bigger, move faster, and build better tomorrows for all.

**Vision:** A world in which each person has the opportunity to love their life and work.

**Anthem:** Better tomorrows, together.

## "Fiercely human"

This is the brand essence, not a tagline. It appears as a descriptor — "fiercely human consulting," "our fiercely human approach."

Three hard rules:

- Never separate "fiercely" from "human."
- Never call things "fierce."
- Never imply Slalom makes people *more* human. It describes Slalom's approach, not a transformation applied to anyone.

## Core values

Do what is right, always. Drive connection and teamwork. Inspire passion and adventure. Take ownership. Get it done. Celebrate authenticity. Focus on outcomes. Fuel growth and innovation. Stay humble and curious. Build and shape a better future. Smile.

## Differentiators

- **Customer love** — we take love seriously; measured across 10 dimensions.
- **Global/local** — local soul, global scale.
- **Our approach** — momentum that outlasts us.
- **People-centered** — head and heart in everything we do.
- **Partnerships** — deep connections, better outcomes.

Additional approved options: privately held; agility your way; do with, not for; no silos, ever.

## Elevator pitch

Start with:

> "I'm a [role] at Slalom. We're a next-generational professional services company that creates value at the intersection of business, technology, and humanity."

Add, with more time: the fiercely human approach, deep understanding of customers and their customers, practical end-to-end solutions, and partnerships with Amazon, Microsoft, and Salesforce. Then the culture rooted in purpose and core values, plus current team and office counts. Bonus: recognition as a best place to work, including Fortune's 100 Best Companies to Work For eight years running.

Bring it to life with one concrete customer story — Virgin Voyages' Vivi digital human on Salesforce, the Chicago Cubs' fan engagement work, or Nasdaq's culture activation roadmap — but prefer a story you actually know.
