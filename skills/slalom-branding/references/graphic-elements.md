# Graphic Elements

Source: *Slalom Brand Guide*, February 2025, pp. 49-60.

## Surfaces and shapes

- Prefer clean white or very light surfaces with black text, Slalom Blue emphasis, and restrained accent blocks.
- Use neutral grays for secondary text, rules, dividers, and UI-like structure.
- Use rectangles, fluid waves, or shapes with exactly one rounded corner. Match the corner's curvature to the "l" in the Slalom logo — scale the letter up and compare. Never round every corner by default.
- Avoid decorative gradients, generic stock illustration, unrelated icon sets, and off-palette colors.

## Slalom lines

Three types, all referencing the fluidity of the logo:

1. **Modular path** — curved and straight segments assembled into background shapes. Build these from the Creative Library vector files rather than drawing them.
2. **Bold single line** — a swooping line mimicking the logo's letterforms.
3. **Macro "S" crop** — the logo's "S" rotated, enlarged, and cropped into a background.

Limit line color to tone-on-tone combinations, or a neutral background (white, black, gray) with one accent color from the primary or secondary palette. Separate multicolored lines with generous white so they do not read as stripes or a checkerboard. Lines can also carry typography, as long as the type stays kerned and legible.

## Color bars

Reference artwork: `assets/color-bars/`. Production EPS files come from the Creative Library — never recreate a bar by hand.

- Colors always run in this order: **Slalom Blue, Cyan, Coral Red, Purple, Chartreuse.** Never reorder.
- One color bar per layout. Never more.
- Never larger than 20% of the layout. Scale the height off the "l" in the logo, at full or half width.
- A framing bar's solid blue line runs the full height or width of the layout, and may sit on any side.
- If the layout background is the same color as one of the bar's swatches, change that swatch to its Dark 3 tone — Purple to Purple Dark 3 `#9488F0`. Change nothing else.

## Icons

Bundled library: `assets/iconography/` (102 icons, indexed in its README). Slalom maintains 130+ approved icons in the Creative Library, plus a website-only UI set (arrows, chevrons, play, search, social) and the two-letter Services icons (St, Da, Ai, Cl, Si, Ex, Dp, Pd, Oc, Op, Su, Ps) whose colors live in the Figma style guide.

- Single-color Slalom Blue or Slalom Dark Blue is preferred.
- Use a secondary color only when the layout is already heavy in blue, and then use that one color throughout the layout.
- Icons may sit on colored circles over images or busy layouts. The circle may be any secondary color but **never Slalom Blue or Slalom Dark Blue**; an icon on a circle should be Slalom Dark Blue.
- **Never reverse colors** — no light icon on a dark background.
- Keep rendered height between 0.5 in and 2.5 in.
- Icons work best paired with text. Choose conceptually so the icon adds meaning rather than repeating the words.
- Avoid overly detailed illustration and stock icons. If a needed icon does not exist, match the existing style rather than importing another set.
