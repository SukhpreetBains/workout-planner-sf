# Weekly status outline

Cover and summary always open the presentation. The sections below follow in order, one page each. Add, remove, or reorder these entries after the user approves the change. Empty evidence means no evidence selected, not that no risks or work exist.

## Sections

1. **Accomplishments.** Layout: `list`. Draws on: `decision`, `action_item`, `milestone`, `deliverable`, `session`. Contains: material progress during this reporting period. Leave out: planned or unverified completion.
2. **Next steps.** Layout: `list`. Draws on: `action_item`, `milestone`, `deliverable`. Contains: recorded commitments for the next reporting period. Leave out: invented owners or target dates.
3. **Milestones.** Layout: `timeline`. Draws on: `milestone`, `action_item`, `decision`. Contains: key dated checkpoints in source order. Leave out: undated or routine items.
4. **Risks and decisions.** Layout: `cards`. Label: risk or decision. Card lines: Impact, Owner, Action. Draws on: `risk`, `issue`, `dependency`, `open_question`. Contains: material open risks and decisions needing attention. Leave out: resolved risks and approved decisions.

## Optional sections

- **Workstreams.** Layout: `cards`. Label: workstream name. Card lines: Progress, In the way. Draws on: `workstream`, `objective`, `action_item`. Contains: active areas and evidence of movement. Leave out: unsupported status claims.
- **Action items.** Layout: `table`. Columns: Commitment, Owner, Status, Target. Draws on: `action_item`. Contains: open reviewed commitments needing visibility. Leave out: completed or cancelled actions.
- **Scope changes.** Layout: `list`. Draws on: `change_request`, `scope_item`, `out_of_scope_item`. Contains: decided changes to current scope. Leave out: unapproved proposals or requests.
- **Objectives.** Layout: `cards`. Label: objective name. Card lines: Progress, In the way. Draws on: `objective`, `action_item`, `milestone`. Contains: active objectives with cited progress. Leave out: activity with no reviewed evidence.
- **Dependencies and assumptions.** Layout: `list`. Draws on: `dependency`, `assumption`. Contains: current dependencies and consequential assumptions. Leave out: already resolved dependencies.
- **Upcoming deliverables.** Layout: `table`. Columns: Deliverable, Owner, Status, Due. Draws on: `deliverable`. Contains: reviewed upcoming deliverables and their dates. Leave out: unapproved proposals and already completed deliveries.
- **Open questions.** Layout: `list`. Draws on: `open_question`. Contains: unresolved questions affecting the next decisions. Leave out: already answered questions.
- **Key decisions.** Layout: `table`. Columns: Decision, Date, Decided by. Draws on: `decision`. Contains: approved decisions made in the period. Leave out: decisions still awaiting approval.
- **Sponsor ask.** Layout: `ask`. Draws on: `open_question`, `issue`, `dependency`. Contains: one grounded request for a needed decision. Leave out: invented deadlines or multiple requests.
