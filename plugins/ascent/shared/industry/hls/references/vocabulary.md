# Health & Life Sciences — vocabulary

Standard terms and acronyms, so extracted findings and drafted
deliverables read as client-familiar rather than generically corporate.
Prefer the precise term on the right over the vague phrase on the left
when a document supports it.

| Acronym / term | Means |
|---|---|
| PHI | Protected Health Information — any individually identifiable health information. Treat as sensitive in every document and tracker field it appears in. |
| EHR / EMR | Electronic Health Record / Electronic Medical Record — the clinical system of record. Not "medical record system." |
| HIE | Health Information Exchange — the mechanism or organization moving health data between systems/organizations. |
| HL7 / FHIR | Health Level Seven / Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources — the standards family for exchanging health data. Cite the version (e.g., "FHIR R4") when a document gives one. |
| Prior authorization | A payer's pre-approval requirement before a service is covered. |
| RCM | Revenue Cycle Management — the provider-side billing and collections lifecycle. |
| Value-based care | Reimbursement tied to patient outcomes rather than service volume, as distinct from fee-for-service. |
| SDOH | Social Determinants of Health — non-clinical factors (housing, income, transportation) affecting health outcomes. |
| CDS | Clinical Decision Support — tools surfacing clinical guidance at the point of care. |
| HIPAA | The federal law governing PHI privacy and security. |
| HITECH | The federal law that extended HIPAA's reach and introduced meaningful-use incentives for EHR adoption. |
| ONC | The Office of the National Coordinator for Health IT — sets interoperability and certification rules. |
| CMS | Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services — sets reimbursement and quality-reporting rules. |
| Payer / provider | The insurer versus the care-delivery organization — keep these distinct; they're not interchangeable even when the same conversation covers both. |

Extend this table as real engagement material surfaces terms not listed
here — this is a starting set, not a closed one.
